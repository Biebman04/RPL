270 Ranked Players League

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The 270 Ranked Players League ("RPL", "we", "us") is a free, hobby tournament league for Electoral College (270) games, run by its commissioner, Noah Bieber ("the Commissioner"). This policy explains what information the RPL website and tournament records collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: noah.bieber04@gmail.com

Information created while you play

  • Match results, seeds, scores, points and rankings (public).
  • Match screenshots you upload to report a result. These are stored in Google Drive and viewable by anyone who has the link.
  • Match-room chat messages, "I'm ready" pings and answers, and their times (public in the match room).
  • Signup, forfeit and commissioner-decision records, with times.

We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not run ads or third-party analytics. We don't knowingly collect your location, payment details or government ID.

2. How we use your information

We use it only to run the league:

  • Enter you in tournaments, build brackets, seed players and keep rankings.
  • Let you and your opponent arrange and play your match.
  • Send match alerts you asked for, by email and/or text message (SMS).
  • Let the Commissioner contact you about your match, settle disputes and enforce the rules.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Text messages (SMS)

If you check "Text me" and give a mobile number, we send you match alerts, for example when your opponent pings you that they're ready to play, or answers your ping. These are not marketing messages.

  • Message frequency varies with your matches. It is usually a few messages per match and no more than about 4 per day.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email noah.bieber04@gmail.com.
  • You can also turn texts off by unchecking "Text me" on the Join page.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the service providers below that send the messages for us.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run the league for us. Each one handles it under its own privacy terms:

  • Google (Google Sheets, Apps Script, Drive and Gmail): hosts the website, stores league records and screenshots, and sends email alerts.
  • Twilio: delivers text-message alerts to your phone number.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of players or the league.

Public league information (usernames, results, rankings, chat and screenshots) can be seen by anyone with the website link, including people outside the league's Facebook group.

5. How long we keep it

League results, rankings and history are kept for as long as the league runs, so past seasons stay accurate. Contact details (email, phone, Facebook names) are kept while you're a player. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

6. Your choices

  • Update or remove alerts: change them on the Join page, or reply STOP to texts.
  • Access, correct or delete your information: email noah.bieber04@gmail.com. We'll delete your contact details and PIN. On request we'll also replace your username in past results with an anonymous label, but we won't erase results that affected other players' standings.
  • Rename: the Commissioner can change your username.

7. Security

PINs are stored only in hashed form. Contact details sit in a private Google Sheet that only the Commissioner can open, and they are never sent to the public pages of the site. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't reuse a PIN you use anywhere else, and don't put sensitive information in your username or in chat.

8. Children

The RPL is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has signed up, email us and we'll delete their information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and post notable changes in the league's Facebook group.
